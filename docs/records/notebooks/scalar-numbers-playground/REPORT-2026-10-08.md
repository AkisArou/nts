## What measuring found, after every test was green

Adding `scalars.d.ts` to a large plain program (TypeScript's own compiler)
exposed three defects that no small test could:
- a 21x slowdown;
- a stack overflow;
- a crash on an intersection-typed generic parameter.

All three are fixed. That program now checks at unpatched speed, with identical
diagnostics, and `test.sh` times it on every run. Scalar-heavy code checks at
about 1.1x. A function of a thousand comparisons that narrow one scalar checks
at 3.2x: that is the narrowing feature's own cost.

## Yours to decide

My calls, made overnight and each listed in `DECISIONS.md` as C27-C41:

1. **Typed arrays stay JavaScript's** (wrap, plain reads). Typed reads would
   put scalars into plain programs.
2. **Bit functions** (`wrappingMul`, rotations, `countOnes`, `toBits` ...), as
   completeness. `wrappingMul` is what the FNV hash now uses.
3. **Conversions are imported from `"nts:scalars"`**, never global, so a
   script's own `const Int8` doesn't clash.
4. **Inferred types are never slots.** `const idx = count - 1` holds -1, as
   in node; passing it to a `guint` is the RangeError. Rounding into
   `xs.push(i32)` happens only where `xs`'s type was written.
5. **`a % d` with a `d` that may be 0** keeps its kind (a claim), rather than
   becoming `Float64`.
6. **`Math.round` of a double is a plain number**, so
   `set_size(Math.round(w / 3), 480)` compiles and is checked at the call.
7. **A fraction literal prefers a double overload**, as Java's `0.5` does.
8. **A result of several kinds is their intersection**, printed `A & B`.
9. **The export rule is nts's build's:** the checker can't tell which module
   is the entry.
10. **Library additions:**
    - `Float80` (Zig's f80, x87 `long double`) and `c_wint_t`;
    - `AsNumber<c_size_t>` takes a type;
    - `windows-x64` became `windows-x64-gnu`, with `-msvc` and `windows-arm64`
      beside it.
11. **`NSRange.location` stays a bigint:** `NSNotFound` is 2^63-1, past 2^53.
12. **A library without `targets`** adds the native four to another
    library's.
13. **Float kinds have `round`, not `wrap`.** A float has no modulus; you said
    "on every scalar" (U32).
14. **TS99011 is a warning:** a callback's arithmetic may abort. The abort is
    your rule (U59); the warning only says where it can happen.
15. **A float `clamp` keeps ±Infinity and NaN**, which are values of every
    float kind.

Still undecided, and listed in `DESIGN.md` "Open":
- the performance half (U26): written widths in storage and registers;
- C variadics (`printf`'s `...` promotes its arguments);
- ranges and sentinels (`@IntRange`, `NonZero`, `NSNotFound`, bit-fields);
- string-length units;
- packaging a library that uses scalars;
- the float environment;
- re-running the sweep of nts's own examples.
